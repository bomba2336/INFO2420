Web Site Critiques

Critique Questions

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information?
  3. Does the structure make sense or is it confusing? Why?
  4. Do the graphics, layout, or multimedia help or hurt the site? Why?
  5. How long did the home page take to load? Was it reasonable?
  6. Was the text easy to read? Did the background help or hurt the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was it well done?
  8. Does the site have a color scheme or look that is repeated throughout the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes?
  10. What does the site do that is unusual or very well done?

Pitchfork

Website: https://pitchfork.com/

Critique

1. My first impression was that some of the images were way too large and zoomed in, which was kind of hard on my eyes. At first I also wasn't really sure what the website was for besides music and popular artists. I did like the menu at the top because it has different sections that are easy to find.

2. The website organizes everything into different sections and columns. There is usually a main story in the middle with a large image, other stories on the side, and a section for the latest articles. It makes sense, but there is a lot going on at once.

3. The structure makes sense to me, but some of the images need to be sized down a lot. The really large images can be distracting and make the page feel a little overwhelming.

4. I think the images make sense for advertising and talking about music because they help show off the artists. They fit the purpose of the website, but some of them are still too large and can distract from the other information.

5. The home page loaded almost instantly for me. I thought the load time was really good, especially since there are a lot of large images on the page.

6. The text is mostly easy to read because it is black text on a white background. Some of the font feels a little small compared to the really large images, but overall it isn't that bad. I also think the simple background works well.

7. The website has a navigation menu at the top with different sections. I thought it was easy to use and made it simple to find the different parts of the site.

8. The website mostly uses a black-and-white color scheme. I think that works really well because it makes the more colorful and vibrant images stand out. The same style is used throughout the website, so everything feels consistent.

9. When I made the browser window smaller, the website changed into a more linear layout. The images also resized evenly to fit the smaller window. I think the responsive design worked well.

10. I really like the font and the overall art style of the website. I also like how simple it is. There aren't a bunch of extra menus or things that make it confusing. It's pretty simple and to the point, and the black-and-white design makes the colorful images stand out more.
